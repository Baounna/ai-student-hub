# AI and Cybersecurity News Revenue Model

## Monthly target split ($1,000)

| Channel | Target/month | Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| Affiliate revenue | $500 | 50% |
| Digital product revenue | $350 | 35% |
| Email-driven sponsorship / partner drops | $150 | 15% |

## Funnel assumptions (editable)

### A) Affiliate path

`Sessions -> affiliate click -> affiliate conversion -> commission`

| Variable | Default | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Monthly sessions to money pages (blog/resources/tools) | 8,000 | SEO + distribution target |
| Affiliate CTR (money pages) | 6% | Improve with in-post blocks + tables |
| Affiliate conversion rate | 8% | Depends on offer quality |
| Avg commission | $13 | Mixed software programs |

Projected monthly affiliate revenue:

`8,000 x 0.06 x 0.08 x 13 = $499.2`

### B) Digital product path

`Sessions -> product page click -> checkout conversion`

| Variable | Default | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Sessions reaching product intent pages | 3,200 | From blog/tools/news internal links |
| Product page CTR | 7% | CTA quality + offer clarity |
| Checkout conversion | 5% | Low-ticket offer ($9-$19) |
| Avg order value | $15.5 | Midpoint pricing |

Projected monthly product revenue:

`3,200 x 0.07 x 0.05 x 15.5 = $173.6`

With email retargeting lift (+2x buyers):

`$173.6 x 2 = $347.2`

### C) Email-driven sales path

`Sessions -> email opt-in -> paid click -> conversion`

| Variable | Default | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Monthly sessions | 8,000 | Same core traffic pool |
| Opt-in rate | 3.5% | Lead magnet + inline forms |
| Monthly active list clicks to offers | 22% | 1 core email/week |
| Conversion on click | 4% | Product + partner offer mix |
| Value per conversion | $12 | Sponsored slot / partner revenue |

Projected monthly email-driven revenue:

`8,000 x 0.035 x 0.22 x 0.04 x 12 = $29.6`

With list maturity (3 months, 4x click depth + repeat buyers):

`$29.6 x 5 = $148`

### D) Sponsorship inquiry path

`Sessions -> sponsorship inquiry -> closed slot`

| Variable | Default | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Monthly sessions | 8,000 | Baseline traffic target |
| Sponsorship inquiry rate | 0.06% | From resource-heavy pages + footer/contact |
| Inquiry-to-close rate | 25% | Qualified niche partner fit |
| Avg sponsorship value | $125 | One newsletter/brief slot |

Projected monthly sponsorship revenue:

`8,000 x 0.0006 x 0.25 x 125 = $150`

## Traffic requirement summary

- To approach $1,000/month with current low-ticket model, target **8k-12k monthly sessions** with high-intent distribution toward blog/tools/resources.
- If traffic is lower, increase revenue per visitor via:
  - stronger affiliate offer quality,
  - better product conversion copy,
  - higher email click depth.

## Scenario table

| Scenario | Sessions/mo | RPV (revenue per visitor) | Est. revenue |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Conservative | 5,000 | $0.06 | $300 |
| Base | 8,000 | $0.125 | $1,000 |
| Upside | 12,000 | $0.14 | $1,680 |

## Weekly review checklist

- Affiliate CTR >= 5%
- Newsletter opt-in >= 3%
- Product page conversion >= 3%
- Sponsorship inquiries >= 3/month
- Sponsorship close rate >= 20%
- Revenue per 100 sessions >= $10
- Top 10 money pages have fresh internal links from news/blog
