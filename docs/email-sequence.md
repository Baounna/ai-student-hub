# AI and Cybersecurity News 7-Email Monetization Sequence

Use this sequence in ConvertKit automation after any newsletter/lead-magnet signup.

## Sequence goals

1. Build trust with practical outcomes.
2. Route readers from content to tools pages.
3. Convert qualified readers to low-ticket product.
4. Recover non-buyers with tool-first CTA.

## Cadence

- Email 1: immediately
- Email 2: day 2
- Email 3: day 4
- Email 4: day 6
- Email 5: day 9
- Email 6: day 12
- Email 7: day 16

## 7-email plan

| # | Subject | Objective | Primary CTA | Target page |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Start here: your AI + CS execution roadmap | Deliver lead magnet + set expectation | Open roadmap | `/[lang]` lead magnet URL |
| 2 | Pick your stack in 20 minutes (student budget) | Push high-intent tools click | Open tools lab | `/[lang]/compare` |
| 3 | Portfolio sprint: ship in 7 days | Trigger execution behavior | Read execution guide | `/[lang]/blog/[money-post]` |
| 4 | Tool decision framework (with official references) | Increase affiliate click confidence | Open recommended tool | `/[lang]/compare/[slug]` |
| 5 | From project to internship interviews | Position product value | Open student guide | `/[lang]/product/ai-career-guide` |
| 6 | Common mistakes that slow student builders | Objection handling + trust | See resources | `/[lang]/resources` |
| 7 | Last call: 14-30 day execution plan | Conversion push for product | Buy student guide | Checkout URL or product page fallback |
